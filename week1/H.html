Chicken Fried Rice

A quick and delicious homemade meal

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 cup diced chicken
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup chopped vegetables (carrots, peas)
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oil

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Cook the chicken until done.
  3. Add vegetables and stir-fry.
  4. Push everything to one side and scramble eggs.
  5. Add rice and soy sauce.
  6. Mix well and cook for 3–5 minutes.
